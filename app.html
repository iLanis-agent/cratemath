Crate Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts a packing job with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the crates the items take, the weight and the trips, the cost an item, and the minutes the load packs. It uses no named constants. The items, the crate size, the crate weight, the van limit, the crate price and the packing speed are yours to decide and read off your own shelves, scales and invoices. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your stock, your crates or your van and does not know whether the items will really fit, the floor will hold the weight or the price will hold, and it gives no verdict that a crate is worth its price or a van is right for the job. Every number here is your own entry.

The crates truth

Crates: the items divided by the items a crate, rounded up, with what the last crate covers.

The weight truth

Weight: the crates times the kilograms a crate, split into trips at the kilograms a trip, with what the last trip carries.

The cost truth

Cost: the crates times the crate price, to the cent, and spread over the items.

The time truth

Time: the items divided by the items a minute, rounded up, in minutes and in hours and minutes.

Crate Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
